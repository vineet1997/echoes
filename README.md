# Echoes design brief

Make Echoes feel like a poster for a spoken-word show. Let one photograph of Aditi performing, one large title and a few lines of copy introduce the work. Keep booking visible in the navigation and give planners the practical details at the end.

## References

- Kae Tempest uses documentary photography and plain labels for live work and videos. Echoes borrows that directness for the opening and the clip links. [Kae Tempest](https://www.kaetempest.com/)
- Es Devlin’s work index gives images and project names the focus. Echoes gives the performance photo and the show title their own space. [Es Devlin](https://esdevlin.com/work)
- Sofar pairs photography of real events with a clear private booking route. Echoes brings that clarity to its enquiry link and format choices. [Sofar Studios](https://www.sofarstudios.com/private-events)

## Art direction

Set everything in Archivo, using the heavy weights for the title and regular weights for the copy. Use near-black ink, warm paper and tomato red for the booking link. Use straight image edges, generous margins and thin rules. A twelve-column grid allows the photo and title to sit side by side on desktop and stack on mobile.

1. **Opening.** The title, the premise, a photograph of Aditi at the microphone and one line crediting her Forbes recognition.
2. **The show.** Two performances that play on demand, with *29* taking the larger frame on desktop.
3. **The artist.** A short biography connecting Locale.ai, Aditi’s decision to step away and her published writing. [Forbes profile](https://www.forbes.com/profile/localeai/) · [Aditi on leaving Locale.ai](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aditisinha1002_i-was-just-23-when-we-started-localeai-with-activity-7342867478894563328-bB8p)
4. **Bookings.** Two rows for the durations and a link to contact Aditi on LinkedIn.

## Preview and launch

Open `dist/index.html` in a browser or run `python -m http.server 4187 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory dist` from the `echoes` directory. The site uses local assets and needs no build. The opening still comes from the same performance as the 38-second clip of *29*. The second clip is *Because Luck Wears Lipstick*. Each clip is under 2 MB and loads when requested. Confirm Aditi’s choice of excerpts and booking channel before launch.
